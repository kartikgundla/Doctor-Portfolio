Generate Admin Login Credentials

This runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. It produces the ADMIN_EMAIL and ADMIN_CREDENTIAL_HASH values to paste into your hosting provider's environment variables (e.g. Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables). Delete this file from your live site once you've generated your credentials, or keep it private — it's harmless to leave around, but there's no reason to expose it publicly.
